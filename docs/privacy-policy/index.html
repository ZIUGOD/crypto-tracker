Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-27 · Last updated: 2026-10-27

1. Who we are

The Crypto Tracker app (“the app”) is operated by Caique Oliveira Carvalho, an individual developer, who acts as controller of the personal data processed under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 – LGPD) and, where applicable, the GDPR. Privacy contact: caique.softwaredeveloper@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

The app has no user accounts and no server of its own. We do not ask for your name, email or any registration data.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Showing prices and sending the notifications you turned on: performance of the service you requested (LGPD art. 7, V).
  • Showing ads that fund the app, through Google AdMob: legitimate interest and/or consent, depending on the configuration that applies to your region.

We do not sell personal data and we do not make automated decisions about you. The app contains no analytics or tracking tools of our own.

4. Third parties

  • Google AdMob: shows banner ads. It may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions. How Google uses data in ads · Google's policy. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • CoinGecko and CoinCap: provide prices, icons and coin data. When the app queries them, the network requests reveal your IP address to those services. A coin's details button opens its page on the CoinGecko website, which is governed by that site's own policy.
  • Hosting of these documents: GitHub Pages, which may record access data (such as IP) in its own logs. These pages use no cookies or analytics of their own.

The app is a native Android app and does not use its own cookies.

5. International transfers

The providers above may process data outside Brazil, including in the United States, under each provider's own policies and safeguards (LGPD art. 33).

6. Storage, security and retention

Preferences and the price cache stay in the app's private area on your device, protected by the Android system. The app allows Android backup: preferences (theme, language, pinned coins and notification settings) may be copied to your Google Account by Android and restored on another device; the price cache is excluded. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. Data held by third parties follows their own retention periods. Because we do not run a server holding your data, we cannot delete it remotely. Messages you send us by email are kept for as long as needed to assist you. No system is completely free of risk and we do not guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Under LGPD art. 18 (and, where applicable, the GDPR), you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent. Since we do not hold personal data about you, we will tell you so if you ask; for data held by third parties such as Google, use their own tools. To contact us, write to caique.softwaredeveloper@gmail.com; we will reply within 15 days. You may also contact the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD). Data protection officer (DPO): the operator, at the email above.

8. Children and teenagers

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date shows the current revision.
