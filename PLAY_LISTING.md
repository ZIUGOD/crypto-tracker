## English (default language)

**App name:** Crypto Tracker

**Short description (52):**
Live top 10 crypto prices, pinned to your status bar

**Full description:**
Crypto Tracker shows the live prices of the top 10 cryptocurrencies by market cap in one clean, fast screen - and lets you pin the ones you care about to your notification bar.

KEY FEATURES
• Top 10 ranking: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tether, BNB, XRP and more, with price and 24h change.
• Notification pins: pin up to 5 coins and see their price at a glance, without opening the app. A small ₿ icon appears in the status bar while a coin is pinned.
• Coin details: tap a coin for a short description, 24h high and low, market cap and 24h volume.
• Auto-refresh: prices update every minute while the app is open and every 15 minutes in the background for pinned coins.
• Works offline: if you lose your connection, the last saved prices are shown with a clear notice.
• Light, dark and automatic themes.
• English and Brazilian Portuguese.
• No account, no sign-up. Your preferences stay on your device.

DATA SOURCES
Prices come from public market-data providers (CoinGecko, with CoinPaprika as a backup). Values may be delayed or differ slightly from other sources.

IMPORTANT
Crypto Tracker is an informational app. It is not an exchange or broker, does not hold or trade funds, and does not provide financial, investment or tax advice. Cryptocurrency prices are volatile; do your own research.

The app is free and contains ads.

O Crypto Tracker mostra os preços ao vivo das 10 maiores criptomoedas por valor de mercado em uma tela limpa e rápida, e deixa você fixar as que mais importam na barra de notificações.

PRINCIPAIS RECURSOS
• Ranking Top 10: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tether, BNB, XRP e mais, com preço e variação em 24h.

• Funciona offline: sem conexão, o app mostra os últimos preços salvos com um aviso claro.
